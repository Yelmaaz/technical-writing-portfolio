## Purpose and Scope
This document provides a structured onboarding process for new Customer Success Managers at Clearpath, detailing required system access, tool training, role expectations, escalation protocols, and performance milestones for the first 90 days.

Customer Success at Clearpath is responsible for client coordination, onboarding, and retention. CSMs are the primary point of contact for clients after contract signature. They are responsible for ensuring clients successfully adopt Clearpath’s platform, achieve their engagement goals, and renew.

**What this document covers:**
- System provisioning and access
- Day one and week one workflows
- Tool walkthroughs and training requirements
- Escalation procedures and severity definitions
- 30/60/90-day performance expectations

**What this document does not cover:**
- Sales handoff procedures (see: Sales to CS Handoff Runbook)
- Enterprise account management (see: Enterprise CS Playbook)
- Technical troubleshooting (see: Engineering Support Escalation Guide)

## Role Overview
### What a CSM does at Clearpath
Customer Success Managers own the post-sale client relationship. A CSM’s core responsibilities are:
- Conducting client onboarding calls and configuring initial workspaces
- Monitoring client engagement health and flagging at-risk accounts
- Coordinating billing questions with Billing Operations
- Routing product bugs and feature requests to Engineering Support
- Driving renewals and expansion opportunities in coordination with Account Executives
### What a CSM does not do
Customer Success is responsible for coordination, not engineering, billing, or contract tasks. CSMs strictly coordinate and escalate issues—they do not perform technical troubleshooting, billing changes, or contract negotiations.
- Modify production data or client billing states directly
- Commit to unsupported integrations or custom feature timelines
- Resolve technical incidents independently (escalate immediately)
- Handle contract negotiations (route to Account Executive)
Escalate issues as soon as you recognize potential client impact. Early escalation protects client trust. Delayed escalation risks greater client dissatisfaction and longer resolution times.

## Day One
Day one is orientation and access verification. You are not expected to handle client interactions on day one.
### Morning
- Meet your CS Lead and confirm your onboarding schedule
- Verify all tool access is active (see [Tool Stack](https://coda.grammarly.com/d/_dyv1tghrpFm#_tuool-stack))
- Complete Clearpath product walkthrough with CS Lead (approximately 2 hours)
- Review this document in full and flag any questions to your CS Lead
### Afternoon
- Complete HubSpot orientation (self-paced, link in Notion under CS Onboarding)
- Review the Sales to CS Handoff Runbook (Notion: CS Playbooks)
- Shadow one client onboarding call with an existing CSM (observation only)
- End of day: 15-minute check-in with CS Lead
### Day one deliverables
- All tool access confirmed active
- HubSpot orientation complete
- Sales to CS Handoff Runbook reviewed
- One onboarding call shadowed

## Week One
Week one focuses on product familiarization and process understanding. You will shadow existing CSMs and begin learning the client portfolio.
### Daily structure

| Day       | Focus                                                                         |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Monday    | Orientation and access (see Day One)                                          |
| Tuesday   | HubSpot deep dive, CRM workflow training with CS Lead                         |
| Wednesday | Shadow two client onboarding calls, review Zendesk ticket taxonomy            |
| Thursday  | Clearpath product self-exploration, complete workspace configuration exercise |
| Friday    | Week one review with CS Lead, questions and open items                        |
### Week one goals
- Understand the full client lifecycle from handoff to renewal
- Navigate HubSpot confidently: contacts, accounts, deal stages, health scores
- Understand Zendesk ticket categories and routing logic
- Identify the five most common client questions in the first 30 days
- Know who to contact for each escalation type (see [Escalation Procedures](#escalation-procedures))
### Week one deliverables
- HubSpot CRM training completed and confirmed with CS Lead
- Zendesk ticket taxonomy reviewed
- Escalation matrix reviewed and acknowledged
- 5 onboarding calls shadowed by end of week

## Weeks Two to Four
Weeks two through four move you from observation to guided independence, as you begin handling simple client tasks while still getting CS Lead support.
### Week two
- Begin responding to Tier 1 Zendesk tickets independently (CS Lead reviews before submission)
- Shadow one renewal call with an Account Executive
- Complete Clearpath billing workflow training with Billing Ops
- Review three recorded onboarding calls in the CS training library (Notion)
### Week three
- Handle Tier 1 support tickets independently without CS Lead review
- Co-lead one client onboarding call with CS Lead present
- Complete escalation certification (Notion: CS Escalation Certification, self-paced)
- Begin reviewing your assigned account portfolio in HubSpot
### Week four
- Lead one client onboarding call independently
- Handle all Tier 1 and Tier 2 support tickets independently
- Prepare a brief account health summary for your assigned portfolio and present to CS Lead
- Complete 30-day review with CS Lead

## 30/60/90-Day Expectations
### 30-day expectations
By the end of day 30, you are expected to:
- Complete all onboarding training (HubSpot, Zendesk, escalation certification)
- Shadow a minimum of 10 onboarding calls
- Lead at least 2 onboarding calls independently
- Resolve Tier 1 support tickets without assistance
- Know your assigned account portfolio and initial health status
**30-day review:** CS Lead schedules this checkpoint to assess your completion of onboarding training, tool proficiency, and initial client communication quality.
### 60-day expectations
By the end of day 60, you are expected to:
- Manage low-risk accounts independently without CS Lead involvement
- Handle Tier 1 and Tier 2 escalations correctly and without prompting
- Complete billing coordination training and handle basic billing queries
- Conduct onboarding calls without co-lead support
- Identify at least one at-risk account in your portfolio and document a retention plan
**60-day review:** Evaluate your account management, escalation handling, and client feedback so far.

### 90-day expectations
By the end of day 90, you are expected to:
- Own the full onboarding lifecycle for your assigned accounts
- Manage billing coordination without supervision
- Handle Tier 1, Tier 2, and most Tier 3 escalations independently, routing only genuine engineering issues upward
- Contribute at least one update or correction to CS internal documentation in Notion
- Identify and flag at least one renewal opportunity to the relevant Account Executive
**90-day review:** Meet with your CS Lead and People Operations for a thorough performance review confirming your readiness for regular performance cycles.

## Escalation Procedures
### Escalation principles
- Escalate early. A premature escalation is recoverable. A delayed one damages client trust.
- Never promise a resolution timeline you cannot confirm with the relevant team.
- Document every escalation in HubSpot under the relevant account before closing the conversation.
- Use `#cs-escalations` in Slack for all active escalations requiring cross-team visibility.
### Escalation severity matrix

| Severity | Example                                                                                | Escalation target                                       | Response time |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------- |
| Low      | Dashboard navigation confusion, feature how-to questions                               | Handle independently or route to Zendesk knowledge base | 24 hours      |
| Medium   | Billing discrepancy, workspace configuration issue, delayed onboarding                 | CS Lead or Billing Ops                                  | 4 hours       |
| High     | Client unable to access engagement workspace, data not syncing, authentication failure | Engineering Support via `#cs-escalations`               | 1 hour        |
| Critical | Data loss, security concern, platform outage affecting client                          | Engineering Support + CS Lead immediately               | Immediate     |
### How to escalate
**Zendesk (Tier 1 and Tier 2):**
1. Log the issue in Zendesk with full client context
2. Assign correct severity tag
3. Notify CS Lead via Slack if medium or above

**Engineering Support (Tier 3 and above):**
1. Post in `#cs-escalations` with the following format:

```
Client: [Account name]
Severity: [High / Critical]
Issue: [One sentence description]
Impact: [What the client cannot do]
Steps taken: [What you have already tried]
HubSpot link: [Account URL]
```

1. Tag Engineering Support on-call handle
2. Update HubSpot account notes with escalation timestamp and outcome

## Common Mistakes
These are the most frequent errors made by new CSMs in the first 90 days. Review them before your first client interaction.
**Escalating too late**  
Attempting to resolve a Tier 3 issue independently to avoid escalating. This delays resolution and damages client trust. When in doubt, escalate.
**Modifying client billing states**  
CSMs have read-only access to Stripe for a reason. Never attempt to adjust billing records directly. Route all billing changes through Billing Operations with full documentation.
**Promising unsupported integrations**  
Clients frequently ask whether Clearpath integrates with tools not on the supported list. Do not confirm integrations you have not verified in the current integrations documentation (Notion: Product Integrations). The correct response is: “Let me confirm that and get back to you within 24 hours.”
**Failing to document client calls**  
Every client call must be logged in HubSpot within 2 hours of the call ending. Include a brief summary, any action items, and next steps. Undocumented calls create gaps in account history that affect renewals and handoffs.
**Assuming product knowledge too early**  
Clearpath has significant depth. Clients will ask questions you cannot yet answer. It is always acceptable to say “I want to make sure I give you accurate information, let me confirm and follow up.” Never guess.
**Skipping the handoff document**  
Before taking ownership of any account, read the full Sales to CS Handoff document in HubSpot. Skipping this creates context gaps that surface at the worst times, usually during onboarding calls.
